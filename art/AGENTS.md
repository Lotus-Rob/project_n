# art/: where the sprites and the world map come from

## Map
- `sprites.toml`: the sprite list, with foot points; a sprite's place in it is its ID, which may change (sprites reordered or removed) until release 1.0.0 and never after · `scenes.toml`: each scene's sprites · `placeholders.py`: procedural art, with the world map's crossings and markers (its roads are ribbons the renderer paints, no sprites) and its painted marks (`stamp_<kind>_0..3`, `mist_0..3`, `painting_*`), each drawn on a transparent sheet with its foot at the bottom centre, a stamp's art kept `STAMP_MARGIN` texels off its sides and top, a painting's ground wash `WASH_ROOM` above its bottom, mist and paintings their own margins (the far zoom draws them from small mips, and the quad's edge cutting their blur crawls; `PlaceholderTest` in tools/test_project_n.py holds every margin, on the placeholders only: an artist's PNG that replaces one needs its margins checked by hand) · the villager and the cultivator are drawn side-on, facing right, with 8-frame walk cycles (`<name>_walk_0..7`), each frame's planted foot moving back one stride (12 and 14 texels) so the figure moved by its stride keeps it still · `atlas.sha256`: written by `atlas` with the atlas, so `lint` can tell a stale one
- `world_map.py` (`pixi run python art/world_map.py`): generates `assets/data/world.yaml` from land forming, previews in `build/world_map_*.png`, with the danger reach, the wary profile, the travel bands and roads forked round the danger sites as `--roads` routes them; the YAML is hand-tuned after and is the source from then on, so a rerun starts over
- `world_map.py --roads` reroutes only the `roads:` and `fords:` blocks over today's world.yaml and keeps every other line, hand edits included; it refuses a comment inside those two blocks; a danger reach and a power-0 band with a danger cost in the file make the roads fork round danger sites
- After a hand edit to `world.yaml`'s grid, rivers, places, `danger_reach` or the power-0 profile, run `pixi run python art/world_map.py --roads` and commit its `roads:`/`fords:` (with `reference --update` if pictures move): the selftest `test_the_shipped_roads_are_the_ones_the_file_routes` fails on stale roads
- The generator doesn't know where a river may rise (high ground, a pond or another river: assets/AGENTS.md), so its rivers start wherever gathered flow crosses `RIVER_SHARE`; each river's source was placed by hand in `world.yaml` after generation, and a rerun loses them with the other hand edits

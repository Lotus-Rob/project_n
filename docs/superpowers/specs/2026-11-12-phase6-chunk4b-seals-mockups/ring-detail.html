Which kind of detail feels right for a seal's rings?

Three directions. Each seal has 5 rings turning at their own speeds in see-through layers. Tick "Tilt onto the ground" to see the layers separate in depth. Click the one closest to what you picture. If you'd mix them, say so in the terminal.

A · Script rings

Bands of invented glyphs between double circles, around a central star. Reads as written power, an incantation.

B · Sacred geometry

Interlocking polygons and star figures, nodes at their points, ruled with fine tick marks. Reads as precise and mechanical.

C · Filigree

Many hair-thin rings, petals, dotted lines and spokes. Reads as delicate and ornamental, like a lotus.
